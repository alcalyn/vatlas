Germany · DE

The German VAT number (USt-IdNr)

Eleven characters that deliberately reveal nothing. The German VAT number is decoupled from every other German company identifier, which is what makes looking one up so hard.

Format: DE + 9 digits (8 digits + 1 check digit)

Example: DE811907980

Issued by: the Federal Central Tax Office (BZSt), on application, free of charge.

The format

A German VAT number (Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer, USt-IdNr) is always eleven characters: the prefix DE and nine digits. The national part never contains letters, spaces or dashes.

  • DEThe country prefix, identical to the ISO code.
  • 81190798Eight digits identifying the taxable person.
  • 0The check digit.

The check digit follows ISO/IEC 7064, MOD 11,10. It catches every single-digit error and most transpositions, and nothing more. A number that checks out arithmetically may never have been issued, or may have been withdrawn years ago.

USt-IdNr, Steuernummer, Steuer-ID, W-IdNr

Germany issues several numbers side by side, and they are routinely confused. Only the first one works for intra-community trade.

The W-IdNr is the change worth watching. Since November 2024 every economically active party is issued a business identification number, and for a company that already has a USt-IdNr it is built on the same nine digits: the first credible bridge between the tax world and the company world. It has not reached the public registers yet.

Finding the company behind a number

This is the German problem in one sentence: no directory can be searched by VAT number. Neither the Handelsregister nor the Unternehmensregister records it, and it cannot be derived from the HRB number.

Handelsregister and Unternehmensregister

handelsregister.de has been free since August 2022 and returns the company name, legal form, seat, representatives and filed documents. You search by name or registration number, never by VAT number. The same holds for the Unternehmensregister and the Bundesanzeiger.

The BZSt qualified confirmation

Under § 18e UStG the BZSt offers a confirmation request that is, in practice, indispensable for zero-rating an intra-community supply. It works the opposite way round from what you would expect: it hands out no data. You supply the name, legal form, town and postcode, and it answers, field by field, only "matches" or "does not match".

If you do not already know the name, it gets you nowhere. If you do, it gives you a record you can keep, and for tax purposes that is the point.

VIES

The European Commission's service confirms whether any German number is registered for intra-community trade. Germany does not disclose the name or the address through VIES, those fields come back as ---. Together with Spain, Germany is one of the two member states where validity is all Europe offers.

The Peppol Directory

One free source is keyed by the USt-IdNr, and it is not a register. In the Peppol e-invoicing network, participant scheme 9930 is the German VAT number, and the whole directory is exported every night. Roughly 10 000 German companies can be reached by their number that way, a small share of the country, growing as the German e-invoicing mandate phases in through 2028, and the only free path there is from a German VAT number to a company name.

Two caveats decide what it is worth. The name is declared by the participant itself, not certified by a register, and being reachable on Peppol says nothing about the VAT registration still being live. Treat it as a lead; the BZSt confirmation above is what a tax audit asks for.

Germany-specific pitfalls

  • A VAT number is not automatic. It has to be applied for at the BZSt, either during tax registration or later online. A registered German company with no USt-IdNr is entirely normal.
  • Small businesses under § 19 UStG can hold a USt-IdNr without charging VAT. Valid does not mean VAT-charging.
  • The Steuernummer changes, the USt-IdNr does not. Moving to another tax office district issues a new Steuernummer and leaves the VAT number untouched.
  • No official path leads from the number to the name. Not through VIES, not through the confirmation service, not through the registers. The Peppol directory is the practical exception, and it covers a few thousand companies out of millions.
  • The Impressum is the most practical source. German law requires commercial websites to publish their VAT number, which is often the only public place where the number and the company name sit side by side.

That shows in the answer itself: a name that comes from the Peppol directory is a self-declared business card, so status stays unknown rather than claiming a registration nobody has verified. And a German number that is not in the directory comes back with an empty name and meta.source: "VIES", empty because saying so beats inventing it.

Frequently asked

The German VAT number in short

How many digits does a German VAT number have?

Eleven characters: the DE prefix and nine digits, the last of which is a check digit computed under ISO/IEC 7064, MOD 11,10.

What is the difference between the Steuernummer and the USt-IdNr?

The Steuernummer is issued by the local tax office for domestic purposes and changes if the company moves to another tax office. The USt-IdNr is issued by the Federal Central Tax Office for intra-community trade and stays with the business.

Can I find a company name from a German VAT number?

Not from any official German source: Germany discloses neither name nor address through VIES, the qualified confirmation only confirms details you supply yourself, and the registers cannot be searched by VAT number. The one free exception is the Peppol e-invoicing directory, where the German VAT number is itself the participant identifier: some 10 000 companies, self-declared rather than certified.

Where do I apply for a German VAT number?

At the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, online or as part of registering with the local tax office. It is free.

What is the new Wirtschafts-Identifikationsnummer?

The W-IdNr has been issued since November 2024 to every economically active party. Its format is DE + 9 digits plus a five-digit suffix per activity, and a business that already holds a USt-IdNr receives those same digits.

Why does VIES return no address for Germany?

Because each member state decides whether to disclose the name and address. Germany and Spain do not, and return --- in those fields.
